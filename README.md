# bootdev_scheduling

General goal:
create an application for shift scheduling.

Seven staff
Three day shifts from 7am until 9pm.
Week days, three in the morning, two in the afternoon
Weekend days, three staff total.


Constraints:
Eleven hour break between shifts excluding a weekent split shift
Thirty minute break when working nine hours.
Where possible, every other weekend off.
Staff may work certain percentages, fifty through to one hundred.
Full working hours is thirty seven and a half hours.
